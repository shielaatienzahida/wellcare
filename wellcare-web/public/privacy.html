Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

Welcome to Heiwa Wellness Center ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and digital wellness services.

1. Information We Collect

When you register or use the Heiwa Wellness Center app, we may collect:

  • Personal Information: Full name, date of birth, gender, address within Region IV-A (CALABARZON), contact number, and emergency contact details.
  • Account Credentials: Username, password (stored encrypted), and account recovery email.
  • Health & Wellness Records: Center attendance history, biometric check-in timestamps, and alkaline hydration/therapy session logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Provide personalized wellness tracking and center check-in services.
  • Log and monitor your daily alkaline water hydration therapy.
  • Enable authorized wellness center staff to assist you during your visits.
  • Send verification codes (OTPs) for secure password recovery.
  • Ensure accurate records for client membership and safety.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Biometric and sensitive credentials are encrypted using industry-standard protocols.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties or marketing companies. Information is strictly accessible only by authorized Heiwa Wellness Center administrators and healthcare staff.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to request access to your records, update your personal profile, or request the deletion of your account. You can manage your profile within the app or contact our center staff directly.
